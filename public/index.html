How it works

  1. 1

    Start a map

    Name it and add your cities: where you live, where you are from, and the places you know well.

  2. 2

    Send one link

    Friends add themselves in about 30 seconds, plus a favorite spot or two. No app and no login.

  3. 3

    Open it before you travel

    Search a city to see who lives there, who grew up there, and what they would tell you to do.

Better than a Google search

The best recommendations come from people who grew up somewhere or live there now. Friend Atlas keeps those in one place, attached to the friend who gave them.

Private enough, on purpose

Maps are unlisted, entries are city-level only, and there are no profiles or exact addresses. The owner can lock, moderate, or delete a map at any time.
